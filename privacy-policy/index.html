ResQmate (DocIT)

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the ResQmate mobile app (also called DocIT, package name com.docit.resqmate, "the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use it. ResQmate connects patients with doctors, shows health readings from a connected sensor device, and provides an emergency SOS feature.

By using the app you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

Medical disclaimer. ResQmate is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment. Readings shown in the app are for information only. In an emergency, always contact your local emergency services directly.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information: email address and password. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider; we never see them in plain text.
  • Patient profile: name, phone number, age, height and weight.
  • Doctor profile: name, specialty, qualification, years of experience, hospital and a short bio.
  • Medical history: diagnosis entries you add, such as title, description, date, doctor and medication.
  • Messages: messages exchanged between doctors and patients in the app.
  • Connection requests: requests between doctors and patients, and whether they were accepted.

Health data from a connected sensor device

If you pair a compatible sensor device, the app receives heart rate, blood oxygen (SpO2), accelerometer and gyroscope readings over your local network. The app derives a motion state (for example "In Motion" or "Stationary") and stores your latest readings in your account so that you and doctors you have connected with can see them.

Location

With your permission, the app accesses your device's precise location only while you are using it, to show your position on the map and to include your location in an SOS alert. The app does not collect location in the background.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your sign-in session and a few display preferences (such as your name) on your device so you stay signed in. This data is removed when you sign out.

What we do not collect

  • We do not use advertising, advertising identifiers or third-party analytics.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To show your health readings, profile and medical history to you.
  • To let doctors you have accepted view your profile, health readings and medical history, and send you messages.
  • To send emergency alerts when you use the SOS feature.
  • To keep the app working, fix problems and prevent misuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your health data for advertising.

3. How your information is shared

With doctors you connect with

A doctor can only see your profile, health readings and medical history after you accept their connection request. Doctors who are not connected with you cannot see this information.

During an SOS alert

When you trigger SOS, the app sends an emergency message and your current coordinates to our emergency service. It places a voice call and sends an SMS containing your message and your last known address to the configured emergency contact.

Legal reasons

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of any person, in response to a valid legal request.

4. How we protect your information

  • All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Database access rules ensure each user can only read their own data, and doctors can only read data of patients who have accepted them.
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and review our safeguards regularly.

5. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your account, profile, health readings, medical history, messages and connection requests from our systems within 30 days, except where we must keep limited information to meet legal obligations.

6. Deleting your account and data

To delete your account and all associated data, email cvineetjonathan@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in, with the subject "ResQmate account deletion". We will confirm your request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

You can also ask us to delete specific data, such as your health readings or medical history, without deleting your account.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit your profile in the app at any time.
  • Location: you can turn off location access in your device settings. The map and the location part of SOS alerts will then not work.
  • Doctor connections: you can decline connection requests. To remove an existing connection, contact us.
  • Other requests: to request a copy of your data or exercise any other privacy right available to you under applicable law, contact us using the details below.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be communicated in the app or by email where appropriate.

10. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at cvineetjonathan@gmail.com.
